# Privacy

Wiki Remaster collects nothing, and has no server of its own.

| Talks to | Why |
|---|---|
| wiki-masters.com and its login service (Supabase) | the game itself: your cards, packs, market and trades |
| upload.wikimedia.org | card pictures, as the game shows them |
| fr.wikipedia.org | a card's article summary, when the game has none |
| challenges.cloudflare.com | the game's own human check, when the game asks for it |

- **Your session stays yours.** It reuses the game's login in your browser; credentials and tokens never go anywhere else.
- **No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party code.**
- **Local only.** Preferences (sort, filters, volume, sidebar) and a 24 h cache of card values stay in your browser's storage for wiki-masters.com, cleared with the site's data.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/KazeTachinuu/wiki-remaster/issues).
